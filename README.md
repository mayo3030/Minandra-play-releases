# Minandra Play

**Ad-free YouTube & YouTube Music for Android — in one app.**

[![Download](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/mayo3030/mt-releases?label=Download&style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/mayo3030/mt-releases/releases/latest/download/Mt.apk)

## Features
- Ad-free video and music playback with background play
- Offline downloads
- Synced lyrics, closed captions, dual-language subtitles and transcripts
- Tap-a-word translation and saved words for study
- AI "Explain this video" and custom AI feeds
- Artist, album, channel, playlist, Library and History pages
- Subscribe, notifications bell, like, and playlist editing with your account
- Automatic in-app updates

## Install
1. Download **[Mt.apk](https://github.com/mayo3030/mt-releases/releases/latest/download/Mt.apk)** (latest release).
2. Open it on your Android phone and allow installing from this source if asked.
3. Requires Android 8.0 or newer.

## Updates
The app checks this repository automatically and offers new versions inside the app. Updates install over the existing app and keep your library, history and downloads.
